Injury-risk awareness

Gradual load increases and built-in recovery weeks, the way a biokineticist would plan it.

  • Periodisation: your training moves through Base → Build → Peak → Taper, building load gradually and cutting it back every 3rd-4th week to manage fatigue before adding more.
  • 80/20 intensity balance: most running stays easy and conversational, with one focused hard session a week.
  • Taper: volume drops roughly 40-60% over the final 1-2 weeks while frequency and intensity stay up.
  • Pace zones: built from Riegel's race-time formula (1977) applied to your own goal time or a recent result, then standard coaching ratios for easy/recovery/long effort relative to your current 5K/10K-equivalent pace.

Sources: Seiler & Kjerland (2006); Esteve-Lanao et al. (2007); Bosquet et al. (2007); Riegel (1977).
